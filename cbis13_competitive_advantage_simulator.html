Welcome to the Simulator

Welcome to your first week of corporate strategy training. In this module, we will explore the concept of Competitive Advantage.

"A company is said to have a competitive advantage over its rivals when it is able to sustain profits that exceed average for the industry."
- Based on Bourgeois OER textbook principles

Information Systems (IS) play a crucial role in achieving this. Strategic use of technology can help a company become better, faster, or cheaper than its competitors.

You will face a series of interactive corporate dilemmas. You must successfully solve each scenario using the concepts learned to unlock the next challenge. Good luck.

Module 2

Locking in Buyers (Switching Costs)

Scenario Briefing

You are launching a B2B Cloud Logistics platform. You want to create High Switching Costs so that once a company integrates your software, it is too painful and expensive for them to leave for a competitor.

Task: Toggle the features ON or OFF to maximize architectural lock-in.

1. Easy One-Click Data Export
Real World Note: Even companies with high switching costs (like Google) must offer data export tools to comply with GDPR/CCPA privacy laws. True modern lock-in comes from deep ecosystem integration (retraining staff, complex workflows), not just holding raw files hostage.
2. Proprietary Integrated Analytics Dashboards that only work within your system
3. Open-Source Compatibility Allows easy migration to free alternatives
4. Complex Custom API Hooks Requires clients to write custom code to connect to you
5. Universal File Formats Saves data in standard .CSV or .XML
