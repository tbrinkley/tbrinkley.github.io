UNDERSTANDING THE "NINES" OF AVAILABILITY (ANNUAL DOWNTIME MINUTES)

A year has 525,600 minutes (365 × 24 × 60). In CompTIA Network+ Chapter 16, High Availability is measured in "Nines" using MTBF ÷ (MTBF + MTTR). Every additional "Nine" cuts maximum allowable downtime by 10×:

99% Two Nines 5,256 min / year 87.6 hours (~3.65 days/yr) • Small Business SLA
99.9% Three Nines 525.6 min / year 8.76 hours (~43.8 min/mo) • Education / Mining / Real Estate
99.99% Four Nines 52.56 min / year 52 min 34 sec (~4.4 min/mo) • Defense / Port / Water
99.999% Five Nines 5.26 min / year 5 min 15 sec (~26 sec/mo) • Trauma / 911 / Power Grid
99.9999% Six Nines (Ref.) 0.53 min / year 31.5 seconds / year • Ultra-critical benchmark
