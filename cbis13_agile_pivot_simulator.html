Post-Launch Analytics

Release 2.0 Results

Launch Failed: Missing Core Features!

Your Release 2.0 went live, but users are complaining that the app is completely unusable! You forgot to include critical Required Features in your sprint backlog. A product needs its foundational elements to work before adding "nice-to-have" features like cosmetic tweaks.

Pivot Successful!

By shifting to Telehealth, you solved the actual user problem. Engagement spiked by 400%, and remote appointments are filling up. You successfully validated your learning and built what the market actually needed.

Product Failure: High Churn

By ignoring the clear data that users couldn't reach the clinic physically, your new mapping features went unused. App abandonment reached 90%.

Instructor's Note: When should you Persevere?

This simulation makes it seem like pivoting is always the correct choice, but in reality, Persevering is often the right move! You should Persevere when:

  • Your core hypothesis is correct, but the feature just needs minor tweaks or bug fixes.
  • Negative feedback is coming from users who are outside your target demographic.
  • You haven't collected enough statistically significant data to justify a massive strategic change.

In this specific lab scenario, however, the fundamental assumption (that users could physically commute) was proven false, making a pivot necessary.
