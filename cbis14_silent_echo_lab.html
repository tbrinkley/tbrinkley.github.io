📜 Mission Briefing

Welcome to the lab. Today, we are exploiting a hardware vulnerability known as a side-channel attack. We won't use malware to steal passwords; instead, we will "listen" to the physical environment.

Your primary concepts to explore:

  • Standard microphones capture unique acoustic signatures based on physical key location.

  • Acoustic-to-seismic coupling allows solid objects to capture human speech.
