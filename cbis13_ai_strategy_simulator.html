Orientation

Your Role

Welcome to the MIS AI Strategy Lab. In this simulation, you are stepping into the role of a Chief Information Officer (CIO) advising several major organizations.

Your objective is to apply core concepts from your Week 1 studies to solve complex, real-world AI implementation dilemmas. You must successfully navigate four strategic crises to generate your completion certificate.

  • Crisis 1: Troubleshoot a failed AI Information System rollout.
  • Crisis 2: Solve the Productivity Paradox to achieve a positive ROI.
  • Crisis 3: Optimize Porter's Value Chain with predictive AI models.
  • Crisis 4: Build a sustainable Competitive Advantage (Moat).

Crisis 1: The Drive-Thru Disaster

System Failing

Briefing

Your fast-food chain just spent $2M installing state-of-the-art Generative AI order-takers. The Hardware (microphones/speakers) and Software (AI voice models) are working perfectly. However, order fulfillment times have tripled, and error rates are at 60%. The cooks in the kitchen don't understand the new format of the AI-generated order tickets on their screens, and the store managers are frustrated.

Action Required: You have budget for exactly TWO strategic interventions. Allocate them to the correct components of the Information System to fix the root cause.

ROI Warning

Time Sensitive

Briefing

Your retail giant has a powerful Predictive Weather AI, but it needs to be integrated into the correct parts of your Value Chain to ensure winter coats are physically moved from national reserves into local stores before the storm hits.

Crisis 4: Building the Moat

Strategy

Briefing
