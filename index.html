Barstow Community College Classes

Scroll through each level to inspect the core Computer Business Information Systems (CBIS) courses I teach—and watch the 16-bit lab terminal update in sync with your journey.

LEVEL 01 • FOUNDATIONS

CBIS 1: Computer Concepts

An introduction to computer concepts, covering hardware, software, operating systems, and the basics of networking and security. This course prepares students for entry-level IT roles and sets the foundation for Microsoft Specialist certifications.

  • PC Hardware
  • Operating Systems
  • IT Foundations
  • Microsoft Specialist
Example Lab: Virtual PC Build & OS Deployment Students identify and assemble physical hardware components, then move to a digital simulator to install modern operating systems and configure network settings.
LEVEL 02 • SYSTEMS & DATA

CBIS 13: Management Information Systems

Focuses on how businesses use technology to solve problems and manage data. Covers systems analysis, hardware and software methodologies, and the organizational impact of technology deployments.

  • Systems Analysis
  • ERD Modeling
  • Relational Databases
  • Enterprise Ops
Example Lab: Enterprise Database Modeling Students analyze a business supply chain case study, design an Entity-Relationship Diagram (ERD), and build a functioning prototype relational database using Microsoft Access.
LEVEL 03 • CYBER DEFENSE

CBIS 14: Internet Security

Delves into configuring, diagnosing, and supporting hardware and software against security threats. Explores methodologies attackers use and the hands-on techniques required to defend local area networks against them.

  • Wireshark
  • Firewalls
  • Threat Analysis
  • LAN Defense
Example Lab: Ethical Hacking & Firewalls Students utilize tools like Wireshark to capture and analyze live network traffic. They identify vulnerabilities and practice deploying strict firewall rules to block simulated attacks.
LEVEL 04 • NETWORK ENGINEERING

CBIS 15: Networking

Prepares students for careers in network engineering by exploring the design, analysis, and support of local area networks (LANs) and complex data communication environments.

  • Cisco Packet Tracer
  • IPv4 Subnetting
  • Routers & VLANs
  • HA & DR
Launch Subnetting Lab Launch UPTIME ZERO Lab
Example Lab: Cisco Packet Tracer Topology Students design, subnet, and troubleshoot a complex multi-department Local Area Network (LAN). They configure routers, switches, and VLANs to ensure secure data communication.

AERIAL TECH • PART 107

FAA-Certified Drone Program

At BCC, we are taking education to the skies. Based on our feasibility studies, we are building an exciting drone program that explores the intersection of autonomous flight technology, supply chain automation, and 3D mapping.

This initiative helps support local operations like the Barstow International Gateway (BIG). Students receive hands-on training to prepare for FAA Part 107 Commercial Drone Pilot credentials.

INTERACTIVE STORYTELLER

Robot Navigation: Atan vs Atan2 Explorer

I designed an interactive scrollytelling experience to explain the critical mathematical concepts behind robotic pathfinding and game design.

Standard trigonometry (atan) often fails when navigating a full 360-degree plane due to canceled negative signs, causing robots to drive the wrong way. Atan2(y, x) safely resolves true angles across all four quadrants.
